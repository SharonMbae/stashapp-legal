Delete your StashApp account

Last updated: 25 September 2026

StashApp is a personal budgeting app for Android, operated by Sharon Mbae in Kenya. This page explains how to delete your StashApp account and what happens to your data when you do.

Delete it from inside the app

Deletion is immediate and you don't need to ask us first:

1. Open StashApp and sign in. 2. Go to Settings. 3. Tap Delete Account. 4. Confirm when asked.

Your account is removed straight away and you're signed out. There is no waiting period and no way to undo it, so export anything you want to keep first.

If you can't open the app

Email info@sharonmbae.vortanafresh.co.ke from the phone number on the account, or tell us the number the account uses, and ask for it to be deleted. We'll confirm it's you, delete the account, and reply within 30 days — usually much sooner.

What gets deleted

Everything tied to your account is deleted with it:

  • Your name and phone number
  • Your PIN
  • Your profile photo
  • Your income figures
  • Your pockets, their targets and their due dates
  • Every transaction you've recorded, including anything synced from an M-Pesa or bank message

What is kept, and for how long

  • Backups. Our database provider keeps rolling backups for operational recovery. Deleted data disappears from these as they cycle, within 7 days.
  • Payment records. If you ever bought StashApp Premium, Google Play holds its own record of that purchase. We don't control it, and Google keeps it for as long as their own policies require. We keep no card or payment details of yours at any point — Google Play handles payment and only tells us whether a subscription is active.

Nothing else is retained. We don't keep a shadow copy of your budget, and deleted accounts are not archived.
